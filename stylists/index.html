Meet our stylists

Find the stylist whose approach and specialties feel right for you. We look forward to getting to know you and your hair.

Tracy

Hi, I’m Tracy! I’m the co-owner of Timberline Hair Company and have been doing hair for over 20 years. Originally from New Jersey, I moved to Portland in 2019. I love creating dimensional color, low maintenance lived in color, and beautiful blondes tailored to each client’s style and lifestyle. I also enjoy creating all types of cuts and styles, from long layers and textured looks to fades and classic bobs. What I enjoy most is getting to know my clients and helping them feel confident in their hair. I want you to feel comfortable and confident in my chair while leaving with hair you love and the knowledge to keep it looking great at home.

Bre

Co-owner of Timberline Hair Company! My biggest passion is working with curly hair of all types, textures, and lengths. I started my career as a barber, specializing in short cuts, but over the years I’ve fallen in love with the fluidity and creativity that comes with longer hair, too. I believe no one knows your hair better than you do. That’s why every service starts with a thorough consultation, with open communication throughout your entire appointment. My goal is for us to work together to create hair that feels like you and works for your everyday life.
